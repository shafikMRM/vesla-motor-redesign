Before it reaches the floor

Five stages, every car.

The certification route is the same whether the car is a AED 30,000 hatchback or a AED 200,000 SUV. Nothing is priced until it has been through all five.

  1. 01

    Thorough inspection

    A full check of engine, transmission, brakes, suspension, electrics, air conditioning and bodywork by our own technicians.

  2. 02

    History report

    Registration, accident and service history pulled and read before the car is accepted for sale. Anything unclear is declined.

  3. 03

    Reconditioning

    Wear items replaced and faults corrected at Vesla Auto Service Center using genuine parts, not the cheapest available.

  4. 04

    Certification

    The car is signed off under the Vesla Certified Pre Owned label, with the inspection record handed to the buyer.

  5. 05

    Warranty

    An extended warranty is offered on eligible cars, serviceable at our own centre rather than a third-party workshop.

Why buy here

What a certified car actually buys you.

Inspected, not just cleaned

Every major system is checked and recorded before a price goes on the windscreen. You are handed the inspection, not asked to trust it.

A documented history

Registration, accident and service records are pulled and read. If the history is unclear, the car does not join the stock.

Genuine parts, our technicians

Reconditioning and warranty work is done at Vesla Auto Service Center by technicians who work on these cars daily, using genuine parts.

The rest of the group behind it

Service, leasing, rental and resale all sit under the same owner. When your needs change, you move companies rather than suppliers.
